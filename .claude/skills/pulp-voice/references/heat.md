## 1. The explicitness dial

The profile and the story note set it.

- **Fully explicit:** everything is on the page, distributed through the scene (§3), with close-ups at the turns and the climax written in full.
- **Explicit with fades:** close-ups at entry and climax; the middle fades.
- **Suggestive:** no close-ups. Micro-texture (§8) and consequence carry the heat.

## 2. The camera: wide, medium, close

- **Wide:** the room and everything else in it. Who might walk in, the radio, the queue, the schedule, the deal being paid off, other people's faces. The cooking-show rule lives here.
- **Medium:** bodies in relation. Weight, grip, position, what's braced and what gives, clothing pushed aside, faces, hands, who's holding still.

## 5. Keep the scene a scene

- **Something changes.** Power, knowledge, a relationship, a number in the story's state. A sex scene that changes nothing was a loop.
- **Voices hold.** Arousal makes a character more like themselves: the bureaucrat stays polite, the brat insults, the shy one loses her sentences (`voices.md`, Pattern 5).
- **Talk during sex carries business:** a negotiation, a threat, a joke, an unrelated conversation that won't stop. The act stays physically present in every exchange.
- **POV can change hands.** One POV per paragraph, or switch at a break. The climax often reads best from one side and then the other. The profile says when the user wants this.

## 7. Porn-loop failures

1. **Genital tunnel vision.** Every sentence on genitals; the room, the people and the stakes are gone.
2. **Inflation.** Size, depth, volume and stamina escalating by superlative: impossibly, endless, gallons, rope after rope.
3. **Stock lines.** *Take it, good girl, so tight, you like that, fuck yes, mine, breed me,* unless this character would say it and it tells us something about them. Dirty talk is dialogue; it gets their voice.
4. **Moan spam.** Strings of *ahh mmm nngh*, caps-lock screaming, stuttered syllables. One written-out sound per beat.
5. **Orgasm inflation and mind-break.** Rolled-back eyes, tongue out, a vocabulary that dissolves into noise, unless the story set it up and it costs something.
6. **Character erasure.** A person becomes a hole or a cock. Epithets replace names: *the brunette, the larger man, his length*.
7. **The loop.** Thrust, moan, thrust, moan. Nothing changes and nothing is said.
8. **Narrator cheering.** *Deliciously, filthy, so hot, perfect.* The narrator doesn't get turned on; the characters do, or don't.
9. **Euphemism cloud.** The opposite failure (§3).
10. **Perfect attunement.** Nobody misjudges an angle, nobody's knee hurts, everyone comes on cue.
11. **The repeated device.** The same coping move, position, line or orgasm beat scene after scene.
12. **Autopilot endings.** Tenderness after every scene, or cruelty after every scene. Match the register the scene actually earned.

## 8. Micro-texture

The camera list above frames the scene; this is the grain inside the frame. Brief, unspotlit, one clause each.

- **Involuntary body reactions:** toes curling, a lip caught between teeth, knuckles white on a bedframe, an arch that wasn't a decision, goosebumps, a flush, a vein in a throat. Toe curls skew female in practice; a man's feet get a passing mention at most.

- **The condom as object:** micro-texture (the wrapper fighting back, the cold tightness, the pinch-and-pull of removal, the knot, the toss at the bin), character dynamic (who insists, who resists), and post-sex grounding (the act turning back into a procedure). - **Smell:** sparingly, and only real things a nose registers: skin, sweat, shampoo, rubber, sheets that need washing. Never "the scent of her desire".
- **Vocalization:** a sound written out when the sound itself matters ("nn" through her nose, a hiss through teeth), mixed with plain report. Too much reads as comic; none reads as silent.
