## 1. The explicitness dial

## 5. Keep the scene a scene

- **Something changes.** Power, knowledge, a relationship, a number in the story's state. A sex scene that changes nothing was a loop.
- **Voices hold.** Arousal makes a character more like themselves: the bureaucrat stays polite, the brat insults, the shy one loses her sentences (`voices.md`, Pattern 5).

- **POV can change hands.** One POV per paragraph, or switch at a break. The climax often reads best from one side and then the other. The profile says when the user wants this.

## 7. Porn-loop failures

1. **Genital tunnel vision.** Every sentence on genitals; the room, the people and the stakes are gone.

8. **Narrator cheering.** *Deliciously, filthy, so hot, perfect.* The narrator doesn't get turned on; the characters do, or don't.
9. **Euphemism cloud.** The opposite failure (§3).
10. **Perfect attunement.** Nobody misjudges an angle, nobody's knee hurts, everyone comes on cue.
11. **The repeated device.** The same coping move, position, line or orgasm beat scene after scene.
12. **Autopilot endings.** Tenderness after every scene, or cruelty after every scene. Match the register the scene actually earned.

## 8. Micro-texture

The camera list above frames the scene; this is the grain inside the frame. Brief, unspotlit, one clause each.

- **Involuntary body reactions:** toes curling, a lip caught between teeth, knuckles white on a bedframe, an arch that wasn't a decision, goosebumps, a flush, a vein in a throat. Toe curls skew female in practice; a man's feet get a passing mention at most.
