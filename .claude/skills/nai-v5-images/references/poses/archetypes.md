# Partner archetypes

Reusable male kits for poses where his body is part of the composition, not just a body type. A named character (Dennis, the office boss, Bane) builds from the archetype and keeps his own anchors; a nameless partner uses the archetype kit as written. Kits follow `character-standard.md`; tags marked `?` aren't in the `tags/` files (§7): try them, and fall back to the prose beside them.

## FAT BASTARD

A heavy man whose bulk does a job in the frame. Most of the time it's a **modifier on a normal position** (`positions.md` gives the heavy line for each one). It becomes the composition itself when `size difference` drives it: he covers her, he weighs on her, or he is the frame.

### The three jobs

1. **Cover (eclipse).** His body hides most of her. Name exactly which parts of her show past him, or V5 deletes her or shrinks him: hands clawing the sheets beside his forearm, feet or soles between his calves, face under his chin or over his shoulder, hair fanned on the pillow, a tail, crooked glasses. Offset his box a little from hers (x≈0.45 / 0.55) so an edge of her survives. From behind, keep her head and hair out of frame: showing them past his shoulder stretches her body under him to reach. Put what shows where it really is: her arms come out from under his hips, her feet between his calves. *Refs 1, 2, 4; FB-02 ★.*
2. **Weight (crush).** His belly lies on her back or her stomach, his chest flattens her into the bedding, her face is pressed sideways into the sheet. The read is the deformation: thighs splayed flat, cheek squashed, fingers clawing. *Ref 3.*
3. **Frame (wall).** He *is* the shot. From below he's a wall of flesh running out of the top edge; in his POV the belly fills the bottom of the frame and she shows past its curve; in a close-up the belly rests on her ass and crowds the crop. *Ref 5, OFC-09, OFC-11a.*

### The face (or no face)

- **The leer:** `grin, sweat, blush, looking down`, with drool as prose (`Drool hangs from man's grinning mouth.`; `drooling male` ◇). Read: he's enjoying it more than she is.
- **The effort:** `clenched teeth, sweat, open mouth, head back, closed eyes, trembling`. Pairs with the Bored/Ignored layer.
- **Faceless:** head cut by the top edge. Prose: `Man's head out of frame above top edge.` (`head out of frame` ?). He becomes a body that happens to her. *Refs 2, 5.*

### Body-part sentences (pick the one the frame shows)

- Belly on her back: `Man's huge belly rests on woman's lower back and ass.`
- Belly on her stomach: `Man's belly presses down on woman's stomach, pinning woman's hips to mattress.`
- Belly between them: `Woman's thighs spread wide around man's belly, ankles unable to cross behind man.`
- Overhang in POV: `Viewer's huge pale belly fills bottom of frame, curving down over woman's hips.`
- Belly in a close-up: `Fat hairy belly rests heavy on woman's ass at top of frame.`
- Covering: `Man's broad body covers woman from shoulders to knees.`
- What shows: `Woman's small hands claw sheets beside man's forearm.` · `Woman's bare feet poke out between man's calves, soles up.` · `Woman's face peeks out below man's chin.`
- Weight: `Woman's cheek squashed against sheet under man's weight.`
- Sweat: `Sweat drips from man's chin onto woman's back.`
