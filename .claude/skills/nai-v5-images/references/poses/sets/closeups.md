## Rules for this set

- **The internal view gets real estate.** A small inset in a wide shot comes out muddy (FB-03). Either give the cross-section the whole frame, or give it half the canvas in a `split view` (ref 11).
- **Name both edges of the crop.** Say what's at the top (his belly overhanging, her skirt) and at the bottom (testicles, sheets, his thighs), or the crop drifts.
- **Bodies only.** No faces, so body kits and no face anchors. The exception is a split view, where one half is her reaction shot.
- **Positions do nothing at this range** (SKILL.md §21). The prose carries left and right.
- **Two skin colors are two bodies.** Say whose color each part is ("lavender pussy", "pale shaft"). With a same-skin pair, use body hair and size instead.
- Anatomy goes in its owner's box; the base gets the act, fluids and prose.
