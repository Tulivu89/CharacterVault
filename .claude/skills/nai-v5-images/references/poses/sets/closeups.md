## Rules for this set

- **The internal view gets real estate.** A small inset in a wide shot comes out muddy (FB-03). Either give the cross-section the whole frame, or give it a third to a half of the canvas as its own panel (refs 11, 15). In Utility Class the tailbone and navel holograms can show a live x-ray cutaway, so a big hologram panel is in-world there.
- **Name both edges of the crop.** Say what's at the top (his belly or testicles, her skirt) and at the bottom (her anus, sheets, his thighs), or the crop drifts.
- **Bodies only.** No faces, so body kits and no face anchors. The exception is a split or panel layout, where one part is the reaction shot.
- **Positions do nothing at this range** (SKILL.md §21). The prose carries left and right.
- **Two skin colors are two bodies.** Say whose color each part is ("lavender pussy", "pale shaft"). With a same-skin pair, use body hair, fur and size instead.
- **Inside views (refs 12, 13)** show nobody, only anatomy: `zero pictured` (allowed off-file), no character boxes, and anatomy and fluids in the base. Describe the canal or womb as the setting: ridged walls ringing the frame, which way the viewer looks, what pushes toward the viewer. Leave out `cross-section` and `x-ray`; they pull a cutaway diagram instead. `internal` ? is the experiment tag.
- Anatomy goes in its owner's box; the base gets the act, fluids and prose.
