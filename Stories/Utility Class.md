### Kerrow Deep
A deep-core mining station on an airless moon inside a gas giant's radiation belt, at the edge of the colonial Rim. It runs under Imperial charter by the **Halvane Extraction Combine** *(an alternative name, Penhallow, is still open)*. About 9,000 crew from every species on the Rim work rotating shifts.

- **Transit Concourse:** the hub where the shuttle lines meet. Crowded at every shift change. This is where the Penalty Frames stand.
- **Shower Block:** the crew hygiene decks. Tile, steam, and drains that never stop running.
- **The Docks:** freighter berths and cargo crews. Therian and Draxian traffic.
- **Executive Ring:** suites for Combine brass and visiting dignitaries. This is the Hospitality tier.
- **Yield Ward:** a medical deck. Clean, warm, and quiet.
- **The Rack:** the utility dormitory. Bunks stacked four high, no doors, lights that never fully go out.
- **Commissary Board:** a public display of every utility's rating and liters remaining.

### The Penal Utility Statute
A sentenced convict stops being *personnel* and becomes *infrastructure*: **Utility Class**, listed in the same ledger as water, air and power. She has an inventory number instead of a name. **Nobody owns her.** Any crew badge with valid access may use her. Humans fall under it easily: in the Imperial Sector a human's legal protection is only as real as whoever enforces it.

### LEDGER
The station's allocation system. It handles booking, metering, ratings, sentence accounting, and "underutilized asset" alerts, and speaks in prerecorded voice lines in a warm female tone.
- Because sapient AI is banned in the Empire, the Combine insists LEDGER is **"legally a very large spreadsheet."** It isn't sapient. It is relentless, polite, and impossible to argue with.
- Standard prompt to users after every scan: *"How was your utility today?"* (1–5 stars).

### The uniform
Clothing is optional. What identifies a utility is the **kit**: a **choker**, **two bracelets**, and **two ankle bangles**, all of which glow.
- **Status lights:** green = available · amber = reserved or restricted posting · red = maintenance, medical or discipline · **pulsing red-amber = Tier 0 penalty**.
- **Mag-lock attachment points** on every piece.
- **Compliance pulse**, controlled by the handler only.
- **Swallow sensor** in the choker.
- **Hologram projectors:** a small display opens **under the navel** (vaginal) and **over the tailbone** (anal). It shows the volume from the current use and her running total for the shift.
