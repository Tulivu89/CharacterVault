# Dennis Moody - Moved Back Home

## Differences from the main version
- First appearance. This story sets his age at 40.

## Bio
Forty. Overnight attendant in a downtown parking garage booth, ten to six, four nights a week. He lives in a one-bedroom over Kaminski's laundromat, and the dryers thump through his floor until midnight. His mother calls every Sunday. He has one friend from work. At two in the morning, after a forum thread, he bought *The Practitioner's Grimoire of the Succubus* for $19.99, expecting the woman on the cover.

## Role in the story
The summoner. Depravitae is bound to him, can't go more than forty feet from his side, and he's what she eats. The cover story is his: "My daughter. She moved back home." Every eyebrow in the building lands on him: a four-foot "daughter" who looks nothing like him and lives in his lap.

He's the one who comes out fine. He gets braver, sleeps better, gets fed on, and thrives.

## Lore entry
