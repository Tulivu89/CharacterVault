## Lore entry
*Short, keyword-friendly version for lorebooks.*
Yumiko is an eighteen-year-old Vulpen fox-girl with a short, wiry build toned by a life of running, climbing, and street survival. She features dusky tan skin, big yellow inquisitive eyes, and a messy bob of vivid orange hair mixed with pink tones. Her large, expressive fox ears flatten when she is guilty and perk forward when she is mischievous, matching a full orange tail that she cannot control emotionally. She wears a simple cream chest wrap, brown shorts, cream leggings, and a thong riding high on her hips beneath her thief belts. Yumiko is cheerfully feral, unashamedly stealing and lying for her own entertainment, treating the protagonist not as a terror, but as the ultimate meat-shield for her chaotic antics.
