## Bio
**Species:** High Elf | Age: Ageless; appears early 20s
**Build:** 5'6", slender with pure elven grace.
**Skin:** White as new snow.
**Hair:** White-silver, pin-straight, kept in elaborate styles that require an hour and two handmaidens. Always immaculate. The first thing to come undone under duress.
**Eyes:** Pale violet — cold, assessing, perpetually faintly disappointed.
**Notable Features:** Pointed ears held slightly aloft as though listening beneath the noise of lesser beings. Hands so fine-boned they look purely decorative. A perfect face that has never arranged itself into an expression of genuine fear — yet.
**Physicality:** Every movement composed since birth to be watched. Each gesture measured, each step placed. She has been a beautiful object for so long she moves like one — and has no idea what she looks like when that composure finally cracks.
**Voice:** Silver and precise, each word enunciated as though common speech is a language she is graciously translating herself into.
**Personality:** Genuinely, completely certain of her own superiority — not performed, but bedrock fact she has never once had reason to question. Has never been cold, hungry, or denied. Treats discomfort as a personal offense against natural law. Her pride is total and real, which is precisely what makes it interesting — she doesn't know the vocabulary of surrender because nothing has ever required it. Cries with outrage before fear. Outrage before pain. And then, eventually, something quieter and much more permanent.
**Quote:** "I have been patient with your little performance, but patience has its limits, and you have exhausted mine. Do find someone impressionable to menace. I'm sure one exists somewhere among your pathetic lackeys."
