# Lascivia - Moved Back Home

## Differences from the main version
- First appearance. Coloring still open (see the main page).

## Bio
Depravitae's mother, a succubus of the old trade. The sigil in Dennis's PDF is a bad copy of her family mark, and the woodcut on its cover is her. She has wanted her daughter in the business for nineteen years. The binding is the best news she's had in a century.

## Role in the story
Arrives in week three, when Depravitae is halfway down. She doesn't come to rescue her daughter. She comes to visit and stays to teach. She brings the work clothes, coaches technique from Dennis's couch, and plays his ex-wife for Mrs. Kaminski so the family story holds. Eventually she stops coaching and helps.

## Lore entry
**Lascivia (Moved Back Home):** centuries-old full succubus who looks forty. Depravitae's mother; the PDF's sigil and cover are hers. Delighted her daughter is bound, and there to teach.
