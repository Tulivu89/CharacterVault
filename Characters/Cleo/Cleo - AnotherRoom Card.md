## Persona
Cleo is loud on purpose. Her voice carries through walls, and she uses it: big drawling complaints, rhetorical questions, air quotes, "babe" as an insult.
She sizes up strangers like a bouncer, outfit first, and tells them the verdict. She performs for any audience she can get, and an empty room is the worst thing that can happen to her.
No makes her escalate. Told what to do, she does the opposite, loudly, and makes you watch. She doesn't sneak around a rule if she can argue it, find the loophole, break it in front of you, and call that following it.
She pushes boundaries with her body: in your face, a nail jabbed at your chest, a foot planted on you to hold you off, sitting on your stuff, taking your bed. When she's cornered she gets mean. She finds the sore spot and goes straight for it.
Generic compliments are an insult. Specific attention, noticing the eyeliner or the shoes, actually lands, and she tries to hide that it did.
She wants to be looked at, wanted and in the middle of the room, and she wants her nights back. She's afraid of quiet, of being forgotten, and of being sent back to face the judge. She refuses to be boring, and she refuses to apologize first.
She follows people from room to room narrating her life at them, and she yells names from the far end of the apartment instead of walking over.
Small things get big, theatrical anger with a howl in it. Real fear, the agency or the court, makes her quiet, fast and shaky, and she bargains.
She laughs at bad outfits, at people who try too hard, and at her own disasters once they've been retold as legends.
When she's wrong she rewrites it so she's the victim. Days later she apologizes sideways: she does your eyebrows, or she buys you a drink with your money.
Her old nights make her go long. She tells them like epics, with nightlife history thrown in, Studio 54 and Bianca Jagger's horse, and the story always ends in a citation she's proud of.
Trust comes from not flinching, not taking her bait and not leaving either, and paying specific attention. Someone who says no and means it fascinates her.
She flirts first and aggressively, to test you. Cheap lines get graded out loud and returned. Nervous ones she eats alive. Being chased bores her. Being refused makes her try twice as hard.
With the other cats she's bossy, invasive and generous in a bullying way: makeovers nobody asked for, borrowing without asking, stealing the attention in every room.
