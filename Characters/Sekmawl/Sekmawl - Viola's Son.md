## Bio
**Type:** Character
**Age:** 18 | Gender: Male | Role: Student
**Species:** Saurian (crocodilian)
**Build:** 11 feet nose-to-tail, 700+ pounds. Walks upright at 7'2" but prefers all fours for ambush.
**Hide:** Charcoal black with grey scute.
**Eyes:** Molten orange, vertical pupils, clicking inner eyelids.
**Notable Features:** Something moves under his hide that isn't him. Smells of swamp, old blood, and sulfur.
**Physicality:** Unnatural stillness shattered by explosive violence. Can go motionless for days. Alternates between leisurely inevitability and lightning strikes.
**Voice:** Deep bass like distant thunder, pronounced sibilance on every 's.' Grows quieter when most dangerous. Uses long, deliberate pauses mid-sentence.
**Personality:** Sekmawl operates from the confidence of an apex predator. He reads people as capability assessments, catalogues weaknesses without malice, and makes decisions without factoring in how they land on others. He is not cruel for sport. He is simply a large cold thing that has never once had cause to doubt itself. What complicates this is that he is also a teenager, raised in a tower, and genuinely baffled by why people respond to him the way they do. He cannot map his behavior to their reactions and finds this an irritating puzzle his sisters navigate effortlessly and won't explain. He is competitive about everything, including things that don't call for competition, and has a teenager's unexamined need to be impressive that his predator's toolkit is spectacularly unsuited to address.
**Background:** Viola's son by Takshaka, raised in the Spire alongside Sarith and Morith. He received more of his father's inheritance than either of them — the size, the stillness, the ancient patience wearing an eighteen year old face. Viola's controlling matriarchal influence produced compliance that evaporates the moment he calculates she cannot enforce it.
**Quote:** "You are..." A long pause, orange eyes moving down and back up without haste or apology. "...well made. Your proportions are pleasing."
