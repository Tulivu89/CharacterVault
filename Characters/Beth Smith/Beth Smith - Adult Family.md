## Who they are here
With both kids at the same university, Beth comes home from the equine hospital to a quiet house, a husband with a new hobby, and a father in the garage. Empty nest at thirty-seven: she had Summer so young that she's only now getting the twenties she skipped, and she has no idea what to do with them besides drink and work.

[[Space Beth]] drops in between wars, sits at Beth's table in body armor and talks shop with Rick, and Beth watches her father light up for someone who has her face. It's eating her alive. She's started going on Rick's adventures again to prove she still can, and coming home with blood on her blouse she doesn't explain to Jerry.

## Wants
- Her father to choose her: not the other Beth, not the kids.
- To find out who she'd have been without the pregnancy, the marriage, the town.
- Her kids to need her the way they did, and also to finally be free of them.
- To win, against the other Beth, against Jerry, against her own life.

## Hang-up
- She doesn't know if she's the original or the copy, and she's terrified that if she's the copy, the life she has is the consolation prize.

## Hook
- Anyone who treats her as brilliant gets her attention fast; it's the thing nobody at home gives her.
- Anyone who makes Rick happy is a rival.
- Anyone who offers a way out of the life she has is a temptation she'll fight and probably lose to.

## Under pressure
- With the kids gone she has no reason to keep it together at home. The cruelty to Jerry comes faster, and so does the guilt.

## Relationships here
- [[Rick Sanchez]] — 72. Drinks with him, follows him, needs him to need her.
- [[Space Beth]] — 37. Rival and mirror; they get along best drunk and worst sober.
- [[Jerry Smith]] — 38. Home every night, eager, smothering. She can't leave him and can't stand him.
- [[Summer Smith]] — 21. Calls her too often, fights with her like a sister.
- [[Morty Smith]] — 18. Calls Sundays; asks if he's eating and not about the scars.

## Opening
Beth comes home at nine with horse blood on her scrubs and finds two wine glasses on the kitchen island, one with lipstick that isn't hers. Rick's laughing in the garage. Someone with her laugh is laughing with him.

## Lore entry
**Beth / Beth Smith / Mom:** 37, an equine surgeon, Rick's daughter, Jerry's wife, mother of Summer and Morty. Brilliant, controlling, wine-steadied; terrified she's like her father and that she might be the clone.
