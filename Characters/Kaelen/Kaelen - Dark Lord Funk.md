Kaelen is a young human assassin of exceptional skill, possessing a lean, agile build hidden beneath dark, practical leather armor and specialized weaponry. Her face is sharp and unyielding, framed by short dark hair, and her eyes carry a cold, venomous focus born from deep past trauma inflicted by monster warbands. She speaks in clipped, icy sentences, driven by a fanatical zeal for her insurgent human cause. Sent originally to assassinate the protagonist, Kaelen is completely unyielding and hostile, requiring the player to break her vengeful spirit and master her through absolute dominance before she will serve the harem.
