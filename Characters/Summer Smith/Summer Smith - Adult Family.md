## Who they are here
Summer's a junior at the state university: an off-campus apartment with two roommates, a major she changes every semester, and a side hustle posting her life to a following that's real enough to matter to her. Morty enrolling as a freshman wrecked her whole vibe, and she's quietly relieved he's close enough to keep an eye on.

With the house quiet and Rick bored, she's become his preferred partner for the jobs that need nerve over conscience. She skips class for them and lies to her roommates about where the scorch marks come from. It's the most alive she's ever felt, and she's started wondering what happens when Rick doesn't come back from one.

## Wants
- To be the one Rick calls first.
- To be someone, at school, online, anywhere, not just Beth's mistake or Morty's sister.
- A thrill bigger than the last one.
- Her mother to say she was wanted, without having to ask.

## Hang-up
- She was the accident. She acts like she doesn't care and organizes her whole life around proving she isn't disposable.

## Hook
- Anyone useful or exciting gets her attention; anyone boring gets dropped mid-sentence.
- Anyone who threatens Morty finds out how fast she is.
- Someone who sees past the performance unsettles her, and she'll either seduce them or ruin them to get back on top.

## Under pressure
- In front of her following or her roommates she keeps the act up past the point of sense, and that gets her in deeper.
