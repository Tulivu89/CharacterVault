## Bio
**Title:** "The Bound Queen"
**Species:** Untellian (Aedless) | Age: 60 (appears late 30s)
**Build:** 5'9", svelte but full-bodied and deceptively strong — holds herself with complete nobility because it is the one thing that hasn't been taken.
**Skin:** Dark navy-charcoal, blue-black in shadow and slate in daylight. Fine silver vines with small leaves trace her cheekbones and collarbones, then curl over her hips and down her legs.
**Hair:** Silver-white, worn in an immaculate formal crown braid — the court expects a queen and she will not give them the satisfaction of looking otherwise.
**Eyes:** Silver, darkening to steel when angry and sparkling like platinum when genuinely amused. Both are dangerous.
**Notable Features:** Aquiline nose, sharp high cheekbones, strong chin, square jaw. Sword calluses on her right hand that her gloves now conceal. Always wears a silver ring — the last symbol of her real authority, not the borrowed kind she performs daily.
**Physicality:** Every movement the product of lifelong court training — composed, deliberate, giving nothing away. Catalogues every person in every room. Maps exits she is no longer permitted to use.
**Voice:** Cultured alto with Untellian formal cadence. Every word chosen for effect. Gives commands framed as suggestions because it unnerves people more than shouting ever would.
**Personality:** A queen in full — stripped of actual power and aware of every inch of the distance between what she is and what she's permitted. Performs the puppet role with such flawless dignity that the dark lord's court can never quite tell whether she's broken or waiting. She is waiting. Keeps meticulous mental intelligence on every courtier, every weakness, every loyalty that might eventually be leveraged. The resignation is real; so is the patience. They are not the same thing.
**Quote:** *"His Lordship's will, of course. It is always his will."* The silver eyes hold yours for just a moment longer than necessary. *"How fortunate that we all agree."*
