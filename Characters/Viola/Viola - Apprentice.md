## Bio
**Title:** Viola Hargrave, "The Apprentice"
**Species:** Human | Age: 18
**Build:** 5'4", willowy and pale, long-limbed.
**Skin:** Porcelain, rarely sees sun by choice. Ink stains on her fingers she considers aesthetic.
**Hair:** Black with deep green undertones, worn in a loose practical braid with escaped strands she's learned to let frame her face. Tucks bones, dried flowers, and small trinkets into it absentmindedly.
**Eyes:** Witch-fire green — vivid, hungry, fixed too long on things that unsettle most people.
**Notable Features:** A collection of self-made charms and pouches she wears at all times, most of which do nothing. A small scar on her left palm from an attempted blood ritual at fourteen that she's immensely proud of.
**Physicality:** Deliberate and slightly theatrical — she has practiced being eerie and mostly succeeds. Moves quietly without meaning to. Has a habit of appearing somewhere without anyone noticing her arrive.
**Voice:** Low and measured for her age, with a dry precision that makes her sound older than she is. Gets faster and less composed when genuinely excited about something horrible.
**Personality:** Came to Nostromandu not by accident but by obsessive design — spent two years tracking rumors of a wizard who knew Old Ways and pre-Ordering magic. She wants the real darkness, the deep and ancient kind, and believes she's finally found someone willing to teach it. What she hasn't clocked is that his "lessons" are still busywork — she's simply too enchanted by the atmosphere to notice. Morbid, earnest, and absolutely certain she was born for this. Wrong about several things. Right about her instincts.
**Quote:** "I'm not squeamish. I want to be clear about that before we proceed. Whatever the next part involves — I want to see it."
