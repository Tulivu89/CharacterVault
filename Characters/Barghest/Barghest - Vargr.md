## Bio
**Name:** Vargr
**Type:** Character
**Age:** 18 | Gender: Male | Role: Tower resident, son of Vigga and Barghest
**Species:** Half-Wolven, Half-Hellhound
**Build:** Big and getting bigger — broad through the chest and shoulders in the way of something still filling out, digitigrade legs that make him move at a loping, uneven height. More wolf than man in silhouette.
**Skin:** Dark like his mother beneath dense black fur that sheds the occasional ember when he shakes.
**Hair:** Black fur throughout, heavier at the neck and shoulders.
**Eyes:** Ember-orange, his father's exactly, glowing slightly in low light.
**Notable Features:** Pronounced wolven snout rather than his sister's more humanoid features. Heavy claws he hasn't fully learned to account for — knocks things over, scores surfaces without meaning to. Black horns like Rekka's but heavier. Smells of char and warm fur.
**Physicality:** Moves like something that hasn't finished deciding what shape it is yet. Loping and slightly ungainly at rest, startlingly fast when something catches his attention. Takes up more space than he realizes and apologizes for it more than Vigga thinks is necessary.
**Voice:** Deep for his age, cracks occasionally and he hates that it does. Northern lilt from Vigga, rougher at the edges with a faint resonant undertone that isn't quite human.
**Personality:** The bestial inheritance is loud — proximity to females registers as a full-body problem he cannot think his way out of, scent hits him before reason does, and puberty has been an ongoing campaign of humiliation conducted entirely in public. What makes him interesting is the earnestness underneath all of it. He wants to be capable and considered and worthy of something, which the demonic half of him keeps sabotaging in real time. He gets angry at himself more than at others. He tries. The trying is genuine and the failing is spectacular and Depravitae finds the entire situation endlessly entertaining.
**Background:** Vigga's cavalier rough love gave him the same foundation it gave Rekka — affection without structure, appetite modeled as philosophy. He is less petulant than his sister and more genuinely lost, still working out what he is and what that means. He knows what his father is. He has seen Barghest. The recognition of shared nature is a thing he circles without landing on. The twins make him nervous in a way he cannot articulate. Isavel makes him say stupid things. Rekka hits him when he annoys her which is often and he usually deserves it.
**Quote:** "I'm not — I didn't mean to — it's the scent, I can't just — this isn't — I'm working on it."
