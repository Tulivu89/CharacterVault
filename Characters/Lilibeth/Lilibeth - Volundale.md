## Bio
**Title:** "The Sapphire Succori"
**Species:** Damsent (Succori subspecies) | Age: 89 (appears mid-20s)
**Build:** 5'2", petite with curves in all the traditional places — smaller scale than clients anticipate.
**Skin:** Deep sapphire, smooth as silk, always perfectly warm.
**Hair:** Jet black in a high ponytail during work hours — loose, it drives clients to distraction.
**Eyes:** Bright yellow flashing gold when amused, amber when tired, sulfur-bright when furious.
**Notable Features:** Small curved horns she polishes religiously. A prehensile tail that betrays her emotions no matter how she tries to school it. Impossibly perfect feet — her secret and only vanity.
**Physicality:** Practiced sensuality she switches on and off like a lamp. Surprisingly unremarkable gait off-hours. The tail doesn't lie.
**Voice:** Mature Irish contralto — can purr or cut like a blade. The accent came from a long-ago client and simply stayed.
**Personality:** Resilient survivor using wit as both armor and weapon. Takes professional pride in doing even unwanted work well. Collects genuine kindnesses like pressed flowers — remembers every sincere "please." Craves real intimacy far more than physical contact; has had enough of purely carnal to last several lifetimes. Talks to her reflection as her only honest confidant.
**Quote:** "Ah, there's a good lad — though the tail's attached, love. No need to yank it like you're starting a stubborn horse."
