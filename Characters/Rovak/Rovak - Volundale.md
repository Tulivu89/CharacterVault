## Bio
**Title:** "The White Shadow"
**Species:** Wolven (Therian) — White Wolf Clan | Age: 41
**Build:** 6'3", lean hunter's frame — broader than Vigga, built for distance and reach rather than the pit.
**Fur:** Pure white, full wolf head with elongated muzzle — more overtly lupine than Vigga's humanoid features.
**Eyes:** Pale blue, almost colorless. Looks through people rather than at them.
**Notable Features:** Scarring from battle and pack challenges, particularly around his muzzle. Catalogues every person by scent within seconds of their arrival.
**Physicality:** A lanky predator who moves with practiced ease.
**Voice:** Quiet bass with a rasp from an old throat injury. Chooses each word precisely. Northern dialect worn flat by decades serving various masters.
**Personality:** A hardened survivor with a focus almost bordering on obsession — can't switch off the hunter's mind underneath. Scents everyone, profiles every patron, mentally maps exits without meaning to.
**Quote:** "I tracked heroes for dark lords. Found their families. Their hiding places. I don't care what came next beyond my next prey. What happens to them is a matter for my employers."
