## Bio
Nineteen, half-succubus, half-imp, and a muse for people who write filth. She games, sleeps wherever she lands, and has dodged her mother's plans for her since she was old enough to argue. At 2:14 a.m., in the middle of a boss fight, a forum copy of her mother's family sigil rang for her and pulled her into a stranger's kitchen in her onesie.

**The binding.** She can't go more than forty feet from her summoner. Past that, it yanks her back. And she has to feed, on him first.

**The glamour.** Sloppy. It hides the horns, the tail and the lavender, but it can't fix four feet of wide hips and thick thighs next to a big pale man. Even glamoured she looks nothing like Dennis. It thins when she's hungry and slips when she comes.

**Wardrobe.** Onesie pajamas with horn holes in the hood. His oversized shirts over shorts, or nothing. Goth frilled dresses with petticoats, fishnets and platform boots for going out. Later, her mother's work clothes: a pelvic-curtain skirt and a small strappy top.

## Role in the story
The muse who won't be a participant. Her plan is to sit on Dennis's counter and steer him into depravity. The binding makes her the one it happens to. The slide is slow: the first feed is spite, the tenth is need. Her mother arrives when she's halfway down. The bad end is hers: one day she puts the uniform on without being told.

She's the reason for the eyebrows. Nobody buys her as his daughter, and she's always in his lap anyway, feet swinging clear of the floor.
