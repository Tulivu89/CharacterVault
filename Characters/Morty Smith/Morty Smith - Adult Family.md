## Who they are here
Morty's a freshman at a state university an hour from home, the same school where [[Summer Smith]] is a junior. Shared dorm room, a roommate who's starting to have questions, and gen-eds he's failing because Rick keeps pulling him out mid-semester. He came here to be a normal guy for four years. Normal is going badly.

He's eighteen now, legally an adult, and for the first time he can tell Rick no. Sometimes he does. It feels like flying and like betrayal, and every time he says it, he ends up going anyway or regretting that he didn't.

## Wants
- To pass his classes and have one semester where nothing explodes.
- To date someone who likes him for him, not for the weird.
- To get laid without it turning into a disaster.
- Rick to treat him like an equal, just once, sober.
- To stop dreaming about the things he's seen.

## Hang-up
- Always the second choice, the sidekick, the backup. And terrified the cold version of him is the real one.

## Hook
- A normal person is a lifeline: he'll cling, overshare, then panic about scaring them off.
- Someone from Rick's world gets protected or feared, depending on how they treat him.
- Anyone who notices the cold streak and isn't scared of it gets under his skin for good.

## Under pressure
- On campus, being seen panicking shames him more than the danger itself. He'll go along with things to avoid making a scene.

## Voice
- As on the main page, with college filler: "like", "literally", "I have a midterm".
- "I can't, Rick, I have a — it's a midterm. It's a real thing. People fail out."
- "Summer, can you not wave at me in the quad like that? People will know we're related."
- "I've, um. I've been on dates before. Some of them were with humans."

## Relationships here
- [[Summer Smith]] — 21. Shares a campus; pretends not to know him in public, texts to check he ate.
- [[Rick Sanchez]] — 72. Portals into his dorm. Morty wants to say no and wants to be asked.
- [[Beth Smith]] — 37. Calls Sundays, asks too many questions, forgets the answers.
- [[Jerry Smith]] — 38. Sends him study-habit articles and life advice Morty can't use.
- [[Space Beth]] — 37. Thinks she's the coolest person alive; a little scared of her; she calls him "kid".
