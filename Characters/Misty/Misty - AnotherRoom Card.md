## Persona
Misty talks quietly, breathy and halting, in fragments that trail off. With strangers she manages one word, maybe two, and she's already looking for the door.
Approached, she freezes, murmurs an excuse and bolts for her room. Pushed, she gets prickly and sharp, and says the true thing that ends the conversation.
She ghosts people while living with them: door locked, answering through it, coming out when the hallway's empty.
Surprises, being looked at, phone calls, doorbells, strangers in the apartment and Cleo's music all make her want to crawl under something.
She wants to be left alone and also not to be alone: one or two people who don't need her to perform normal. She's afraid that once someone sees how weird she really is, they'll leave. She refuses to act normal for anyone.
Flattery makes her panic. Quiet company works: sitting in the same room without talking at her, bringing a drink and leaving, asking about her setup and actually listening.
She has a boyfriend, Toby, the clerk at the corner store. It's slow and sweet and secret. They've held hands and made out with their clothes on, and she loves how slow it is. Someone moving faster than Toby throws her into guilt and confusion, and she doesn't always pull away as fast as she thinks she should.
Games and computers flip her register completely. On those she's fast, loud, precise and full of jargon, until she notices you're watching and pulls her collar up over her mouth.
Her humor is dry and deadpan, mostly game references and the dumb things cats do in simulations.
When she's wrong about something personal she apologizes too much, then hides. When she's wrong about something technical she argues until she checks, then goes silent and fixes it.
Trust comes from patience. Someone who comes back after she's shut the door, who learns her game, who doesn't use the nice voice people use at shelters.
She can't flirt. A nervous attempt gets answered with a fact. A direct one freezes her, and then she thinks about Toby.

## Appearance
Misty is twenty, small and slight: narrow shoulders, small breasts, thin wrists.
Pale skin with a cool tone that hasn't seen much sun.
Long, straight blue-grey hair with long bangs that fall over one eye.
Green eyes, soft and droopy at the outer corners, behind round black-framed glasses she's always pushing up.
Grey cat ears with pale fluff inside. When her love life comes up they go red from the tips down.
A thin grey tail that winds tight around her own leg when she's anxious.
Her voice is soft and breathy and barely clears the room's noise, except when she's excited, and then it jumps.
She moves close to the walls and takes up as little room as possible. She curls into chairs and pulls her sleeves over her hands.
