## Lore entry
*Short, keyword-friendly version for lorebooks.*
Maeve is a twenty-seven-year-old Pantheren Beastkin with golden-tan skin, long straight black hair, black cat ears, a slender cat tail, and striking yellow cat eyes. Her build is soft and voluptuous, curvier than the fighters in the camp, and she wears thick, coarse-woven merchant wools cut low to display her assets. She speaks with a heavy, rolling Scottish accent, bringing a sharp, calculating mind to the warband's logistical operations. Maeve is an absolute pragmatist who used her feminine wiles to survive captivity under a rival faction, and she views her transition to the protagonist's harem as a simple business arrangement, quickly making herself indispensable by negotiating prices, managing grain, and organizing the army's supply lines.
