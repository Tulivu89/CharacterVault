**Species:** Wolven (Therian) | Age: 34

**Hair:** Dark blue-grey fur/hair kept long and wild, spine-length. Wolf ears in constant motion.
**Eyes:** Amber-yellow predator focus.
**Notable Features:** Jagged scar from brow through cheek. Bite marks on shoulder/neck. Missing ear tip. Claw maps across her torso — "each one a lesson written in meat." Elongated canines. Sharp nails. Persistent musk of bark, wine, and she-wolf.

**Personality:** Vice-driven hedonist and accidental philosopher. Drinks hard, fights harder, fucks hardest. Aggressively direct about body functions, death, and desire. Performs the barbarian while reading everyone's weaknesses. Secretly starving for pack and violently unwilling to admit it.
**Quote:** "You build walls to keep wolf out. Then walls to keep wolf in — call it 'home.' Me? I shit where I want and sleep under stars. Who's trapped, wall-builder?"
