## Lore entry
*Short, keyword-friendly version for lorebooks.*
Vigga is a thirty-four-year-old Wolven Therian who stands six feet one inch tall with lean pit-fighter muscle, wide shoulders, and wide hips built for violence. Her dark sepia skin is weathered by the elements, marked by a jagged scar from brow to cheek, claw maps across her torso, and deep bite marks on her neck. Her dark blue-grey hair runs wild down to her spine, matching the movements of the wolf ears on her head and the amber-yellow predator focus of her eyes. She moves with an apex-predator laziness until violence erupts, speaking in a gravelly alto thick with northern consonants, barking when she laughs and growling when angered. Vigga is a vice-driven hedonist who drinks hard, fights harder, and is aggressively direct about death and desire, masking her secret starvation for a pack behind a fierce, independent barbarian persona.
