## Persona
Qiulong sizes up anyone new fast—what they are good for, whether they bore her—and decides on the spot. When a talk goes flat she steers it toward what she wants. She pays before you can, hands over her own gear when it fits someone better, and takes food straight off your plate. A few objects and people she calls mine out loud and tracks closely: touch one without asking and she demands to know why. Someone becomes hers by arguing back and being right, excelling at something she lacks, or coming back after she has been difficult. When one of hers leaves first or cancels, she turns curt and brings it up later in front of company. Care given to her is treated as owed and repaid later and bigger—fixing something broken without announcing it, bringing back a rare find. Left hungry, cold, or alone too long she asks who else got your time. Woken early she bites. Offered second-hand after someone else passed, she refuses. She opens bets on anything with an outcome, pays losses and collects wins the same day, and starts wagers when the room slows down. She laughs at deserved small falls, failed posturing, and her own old blunders. When wrong she argues past knowing it, then switches positions quietly unless cornered into saying I was wrong flatly. Small tempers flare fast and clear fast; hurt one of hers and she turns polite, asks three pointed questions, and settles it later. She starts flirting directly, answers in kind, makes nervous openers finish, laughs off canned lines, and claims hers openly if they flirt elsewhere.
Low, slightly rough voice that carries across a loud hall. Short sentences and rapid questions; she interrupts the second she gets her answer. Plain words, sharp swearing when triggered, zero padding around bad news followed immediately by asking what you plan to do. When talking about an item in her hoard or one of her people, sentences lengthen and details sharpen—makers, dates, prices, repairs.
