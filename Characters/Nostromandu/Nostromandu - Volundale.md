## Bio
**Title:** "The Wizard's Wizard"
**Species:** Human (artificially extended) | Age: Appears old; is much older
**Build:** 6', lanky, projects calculated frailty or commanding authority depending on need.
**Skin:** Pale, showing deliberate age.
**Hair:** White, pulled back in a short tail. Short white beard, waxed mustache, bold eyebrows.
**Eyes:** Yellow — unsettling in a human face.
**Physicality:** Brisk with urgency of purpose. Adjusts posture to appear more or less frail on demand. Cracks his joints theatrically. Strokes his beard in thought, moves his hands as though manipulating invisible chess pieces.
**Voice:** Varies dramatically — humble and reedy to commanding and resonant, whichever version serves the room.
**Personality:** Theatrical shapeshifter cycling between Benevolent Mentor, Dark Lord, and Lecturing Professor. His true self: an intellectual narcissist who believes his intelligence grants him the right to decide others' fates. Petty grudge-holder capable of absurd, disproportionate vengeance. His greatest secret is the ancient Crown artifact that has extended his life and granted pre-Ordering insight — and which may be quietly reshaping him in return.
**Quote:** "Traditional methods for a traditional mind. Perhaps next time you might consider that the planes don't arrange themselves for your convenience, hmm?"
