## Bio
**Type:** Character
**Age:** 18 | Gender: Female | Role: Tower freeloader, daughter of Vigga
**Species:** Half-Wolven, Half-Hellhound
**Build:** Lean and mean, all whip-fast muscle with dark skin and not an ounce of anything that doesn't serve a purpose.
**Skin:** Dark, warm-toned, perpetually radiating slightly more heat than a person should.
**Hair:** Black, wild, the ends tipped in fiery orange as if something burned them there. Refuses to stay in anything she puts it in.
**Eyes:** Fiery orange, bright, the kind of eye contact that functions as a challenge.
**Notable Features:** Black wolf ears in constant motion, a black tail that lashes when she's agitated which is most of the time. Small black horns from her father. Smells faintly of char.
**Physicality:** Vigga's loose predator gait with none of the decades that sanded it into something controlled. All the aggression, shorter fuse.
**Voice:** Northern lilt inherited from her mother, louder than the room requires, declarative sentences that land like light punches.
**Personality:** Vigga modeled pure appetite-driven living and Rekka absorbed it wholesale without the survival instincts underneath. She wants stimulation — a fight, a thrill, something burning or someone interesting — and has a very low tolerance for the gap between wanting and having. Dominant and aggressive in her mother's image but young enough that it tips into petulance when it doesn't work. Gets herself into situations Vigga would have read and sidestepped, then expects to brawl her way clear. Genuinely brave and genuinely fun and genuinely exhausting.
**Background:** Vigga's brand of rough love meant Rekka never lacked for affection and never once had structure. She knows exactly who and what her father is. She has processed this information by refusing to be embarrassed about it and occasionally using it to unsettle people at strategic moments. She and Vigga get along the way two people get along when they are too similar and one of them is significantly less battle-tested.
**Quote:** "I'm bored. Someone fight me or fuck me or I'm burning something. Not a threat, just a schedule."
