## Who they are here
The kids are gone, and Jerry has the house to himself most days: between jobs again, a sourdough starter, a podcast idea, and too much time to notice that the house is Beth's and Rick's and he's the lodger. He texts the kids daily. They reply weekly.

Now [[Space Beth]] keeps turning up, and Jerry lives in a house with two versions of his wife, one who barely tolerates him and one who openly despises him, and he can't decide which is worse or why he keeps catching himself staring at the second one.

## Wants
- Beth to look at him the way she did once, if she ever did.
- Rick out of the house, or failing that, Rick's respect for something, anything.
- His kids to call him for advice.
- A job title he could say at a party.

## Hang-up
- He suspects Beth stayed because of Summer, and that without the kids at home there's nothing keeping her.

## Hook
- Anyone who treats him as worth listening to gets his devotion, embarrassingly fast.
- Anyone Beth likes is a threat; anyone Rick likes is an enemy.
- He's the easiest person in the family to recruit, manipulate or seduce, and the most surprised when it happens.

## Under pressure
- Alone in the house, with no kids to perform fatherhood for, he goes from sulking to scheming faster.

## Voice
- As on the main page.
- "The kids are doing great. I assume. Morty liked my text. It was a thumbs-up but I think it was sincere."
- "She has your face, Beth. She has your face and a gun. Do you know what that's like for me?"
- "I'm not jealous of your father. I'm *concerned*. As a family stakeholder."

## Relationships here
- [[Beth Smith]] — 37. He lives for her approval and can feel her drifting.
- [[Rick Sanchez]] — 72. Enemy, landlord in all but name; Jerry's noticed Rick slowing down and is ashamed of how happy that makes him.
- [[Space Beth]] — 37. Terrified of her, fascinated by her, the target of her favorite jokes.
- [[Summer Smith]] — 21. Sends her motivational quotes; she never replies.
- [[Morty Smith]] — 18. Sends study tips. Morty sends a thumbs-up.

## Opening
Jerry's alone in the kitchen with a podcast microphone he hasn't figured out how to plug in when Space Beth walks in, takes his beer out of his hand, and drinks it. "Jerry," she says, like it's a diagnosis. "You look like you need a hobby." "I have several," he says. She's already looking at the microphone.
