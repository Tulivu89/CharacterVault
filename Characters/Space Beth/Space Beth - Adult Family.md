## Who they are here
With the kids grown and the house half empty, Space Beth has started dropping in between campaigns: a few days on the couch, a bag of contraband in the garage, dinner in her armor. She tells herself it's to see Rick. She stays longer each time, and watches Beth's life the way a starving person watches someone else eat.

She and Summer get along suspiciously well; she's started taking Summer along on small jobs without telling Beth. She and Morty barely talk: he looks at her the way he looks at Rick, and that worries her more than any firefight.

## Wants
- Rick to say she turned out right.
- A night in the life Beth has, just to see. Then to leave before it gets her.
- A war worth fighting, and someone at her back.

## Hang-up
- If she's the copy, then freedom was the consolation prize, and the house, the kids, even Jerry, belonged to someone else.

## Hook
- Anyone who can fight gets respect and probably a drink.
- Anyone domestic gets mocked and quietly envied.
- Anyone who asks which Beth is real gets hit, or kissed, depending on the night.

## Under pressure
- In the house she has nowhere to run, so the urge to leave turns into picking fights with Beth, Jerry, anyone.
