# Kerrow Deep
> On a Rim mining station, convicts are reclassified as infrastructure for public use and work off sentences measured in liters.

*Untagged lines are canon. **[draft]** marks proposals waiting on approval.*

## Scope
- Every character in this module is an adult. The Penal Utility Statute applies only to convicts 18 and older, and every station user is an adult.
- Users are sapient only. Vreth drones and warriors are non-sapient and never take part; they appear only as labor and security. Vreth agents are fully sapient.
- Any Cephalite in sexual content has a clearly adult body.
- The vault's own scope (its system prompt or `CLAUDE.md`) applies. This note adds setting rules; it never widens scope.

## Premise
Kerrow Deep is a deep-core mining station on an airless moon inside a gas giant's radiation belt, at the edge of the colonial Rim. The **Halvane Extraction Combine** runs it under Imperial charter. About 9,000 crew from every species on the Rim work rotating shifts there.

Under the **Penal Utility Statute**, a sentenced convict stops being *personnel* and becomes *infrastructure*, listed in **Utility Class** in the same ledger as water, air and power. She, or he, gets an inventory number instead of a name. **Nobody owns a utility.** Any crew badge with valid access may use one. A sentence is measured in liters of ejaculate, and the station keeps the count.

## Tone
**Banality, brutality, black comedy.** The system is polite and bureaucratic, and what it does to utilities is brutal. The comedy lives in the gap between those two things, and nobody finds it funny except the reader and the overseers. Build up to things: open on routine, then the encounters, one or two at a time.

## Core rules

### LEDGER
- The station's allocation system: booking, metering, ratings, sentence accounting, and "underutilized asset" alerts. It speaks in prerecorded voice lines in a warm female tone.
- Sapient AI is banned in the Empire, so the Combine insists LEDGER is **"legally a very large spreadsheet."** It isn't sapient. It is relentless, polite, and impossible to argue with.
- After every scan it asks the user: *"How was your utility today?"* (1–5 stars).

### The kit
Clothing is optional. What marks a utility is the **kit**, and all of it glows.
- **Female utilities:** choker, two bracelets, two ankle bangles.
- **Male utilities:** the same, plus the **utility ring** (see below).
- **Status lights:** green = available · amber = reserved or restricted posting · red = maintenance, medical or discipline · **pulsing red-amber = Tier 0 penalty**.
- **Mag-lock points** on every piece. **Compliance pulse**, handler only. **Swallow sensor** in the choker.
- **Holograms.** A display opens **under the navel** (vaginal deposits) and **over the tailbone** (anal). Each shows the current use's volume and the shift total. Deposits are logged **on entry**, regardless of leakage. Oral deposits count only if swallowed, logged by the choker.
- **Cross-section view.** On a user's request, any display flips to a live x-ray cutaway in translucent blue: the anatomy, the penetration, and the ejaculation as it happens. Users love it. Overseers call it educational.

### The utility ring
- A glowing ring worn at the base of a male utility's cock and balls. It shows the same status lights as the rest of the kit, and it has a display of its own, projected over the pubis and facing out.
- **Output metering.** The ring measures the utility's own ejaculate. Output delivered to a client during a badged use (in her, on her, or swallowed by him) is credited **1:1** toward his sentence. Output with no badged client is logged as *unbillable* and credits nothing.
- **Ring hold.** The handler can lock out the utility's orgasm, like the compliance pulse. Users can't. Users may *request* a hold, and LEDGER passes the request to the handler.
- Male utilities take deposits the same way women do: oral through the choker, anal through the tailbone display.

### Ratings and tiers
Ratings use a 7-day rolling average.

| Tier | Posting | Requirement | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| **0: Penalty Frame** | Transit Concourse | Assigned for a violation | Locked in a fixed posture set by LEDGER. Users **scan and go**. No cleanup unless a user asks. Highest volume, no dignity. |
| **1: Concourse Stand** | Transit Concourse | 3.0+ average, 3 days without a violation | Tethered, not locked. Can shift position, speak, ask for cleanup. |
| **2: Shower Block** | Crew hygiene decks | 3.5+ | Untethered inside the block. One user at a time. Users are cleaner and **take their time**. |
| **3: Hospitality** | Executive Ring | 4.2+ sustained 14 days | Clothes, a room, conversation. **Multiplier while the rating holds**: ×1.5 at 4.5+, ×2 at 4.8+. The politics happen here. |

- **Violations:** refusal, injuring a user, sabotaging equipment, deliberate waste of deposit. A user complaint triggers a review. Violations add liters and can drop a utility to Tier 0 for a set number of days.
- **Exceptional yield clause.** After a record deposit, the overseer of record may commute the rest of a Tier 0 posting.

### Frame postures
The Penalty Frame reads the badge and adjusts for the user's species.
- **Standard:** bent over a padded crossbar at hip height; wrists locked under the bar, ankles locked wide to floor plates; head through a front yoke at standing height. Both ends are reachable, and the holograms face out.
- **Canine:** hips raised, feet back and wide, "for tie geometry." Overseers set it before the dock shift.
- **Porcine:** the bar lowers, knee pads slide out, and the plates drag her feet back until she's on all fours at bench height.
- **Equine [draft]:** the bar rises onto a padded belly cradle so the user can mount from behind and above.
- **Cephalite [draft]:** lowered, and the cutaway comes on by default.

### Yield Program
- Outside the ladder. **Assigned as punishment:** credited only for the sire's volume; no slate-clear. **Volunteered:** one pregnancy carried to term clears the entire sentence.
- LEDGER picks a compatible sire. The Combine owns the offspring contract. Overseers hand out the brochure regularly: lilac, glossy, *ONE CHOICE. ZERO LITERS.*, in a very friendly font.
- **[draft] Male utilities:** a volunteer sire contract. LEDGER matches him with Yield volunteers or Combine breeding contracts. Ten confirmed conceptions clear the sentence.

### Overseers
- A contracted **Utility Compliance Officer** handles 6–10 utilities. Overseers control postings, privileges (meals, sleep, comms, sedation, clothing), violations (written or buried), the compliance pulse and the ring hold. They can petition for early release. Their pay is tied to caseload metrics.
- Professional conduct says an overseer doesn't use their own caseload. When one does, it's an event.

## The wider world
- Kerrow sits on the Rim of the **Imperial Sector**: Aedling Empire at the core, colonial Rim, deep void beyond. A human's legal protection is only as real as whoever enforces it, which is why the Statute catches so many humans.
- The species are summarized under Peoples. If your vault has its own Imperial Sector, you can drop this module into it.

## Places
- **Transit Concourse:** the hub where six shuttle lines meet under a scratched polymer dome, with the gas giant filling half the sky. Crowded at every shift change. A noodle cart by Line Four. The three Penalty Frames stand on a plinth between Lines Two and Three, and the Concourse Stands line the platforms.
- **Shower Block:** the crew hygiene decks. Tile, steam, and drains that never stop running.
- **The Docks:** freighter berths and cargo crews. Therian and Draxian traffic. The dock horn at 1300 lets out the busiest shift.
- **Executive Ring:** suites for Combine brass and visiting dignitaries. The Hospitality tier.
- **Yield Ward:** a medical deck. Clean, warm, and quiet.
- **The Rack:** the utility dormitory. Bunks stacked four high, no doors, lights at forty percent by day and twenty at night. The kit goes red at shift end, so utilities can't be badged there.
- **Commissary Board:** a public display, the length of a wall, of every utility's rating and liters remaining. Record holders show in gold for seven days.
- **Wallow deck:** the porcine Terran hygiene deck. Temperature-controlled mud. Porcine Terrans don't use the Shower Block.
- **Med-deck pharmacy:** sells lidocaine gel to the Rack at four credits.

## Peoples
*Every species below is sapient. Naming follows real-world languages, anglicized, one family per species.*
- **Human:** everywhere, unmarked, legally fragile. Names: any Earth language, mixed.
- **Aedling:** the imperial class. White-spectrum skin, black sclera, pupils that match the hair. Haughty, bewildered by resistance. **Transaction tattoos are a cultural practice**, applied by custom and not written by the body. They record standing arrangements, not single uses: a penal utility carries one inscription for her whole service, tattooed on the inner thighs at intake. Names: Latin and Norman French.
- **Aedelvass:** warm brown skin, and hair that fades into luminescent ends. Their glow shows fear and arousal and can't be suppressed, and at climax it flares in a full-body pulse. Often undocumented. Names: Welsh and Cornish.
- **Therian:** engineered settlers turned second-class. Females are human with species markers (ears, eyes, tail). Males are full hybrids: canine (heavy, pack-bound, knotting), feline (lean, barbed), reptilian (heat-dependent), equine (massive). Names: Slavic.
- **Draxian:** competitive biology. Males never stop growing, and the young ones fight to the death. Females are soldiers, pilots and politicians. Names: Turkic and Mongolic.
- **Cephalite:** small, soft, brightly colored, with prehensile tentacular hair. Obsessive technical specialists. Male output is a spermatophore. Names: Finnish and Estonian.
- **Vreth:** a hive species. Drones and warriors are non-sapient, used only as labor and security, and never in sexual content. **Agents**, bred from a donor species, are fully sapient. A rare few have separated from their queen. Names: a brood designation transliterated from Sumerian, plus a surname the agent picks.
- **Porcine Terran:** uplifted Terran pigs, made a sapient Terran species by one scientist's tweak on the nearest-to-human brain on Earth. Quadruped boars and sows. They speak a developed language of grunts, clicks and snorts that few non-pigs understand, so crews often have a translator. Corkscrew anatomy and huge yield. Names: short English nicknames given by human crews (Hoss, Bess, Duke).
- **Remnant Synthetic:** sapient machines from before the AI ban, tolerated on the Rim. Yield 0 mL, ruling under review.

## Conflict axes
- **Pride vs. arithmetic:** fight the system and pay in liters, or play it and pay in self.
- **Utility vs. utility:** the board is public, privileges are scarce, and someone is always informing.
- **Overseer vs. metrics:** care for the caseload, or bill it.
- **The Combine's secrets:** what really happens in the shafts, and who gets reclassified to keep it quiet.

## Arc
1. **Intake.** Reclassification, the kit, the number, the first posting.
2. **Routine.** The Frame or the Stand. Learning the regulars, the rates, the overseer.
3. **The turn.** One use changes how the utility plays: a record, a humiliation, a regular she has to escape.
4. **The climb.** Ratings as armor. Shower Block, then Hospitality, and the politics there.
5. **The way out.** Release, the Yield Program, an overseer's petition, or the secret that buys freedom.

- **Client.**
  - **Fixed:** adult station crew or a visitor with a valid badge.

- **Overseer.**
  - **Fixed:** a contracted Utility Compliance Officer, paid on metrics.
  - **Open:** species, gender, style (cheerful, disciplinary, ex-utility, or your own), and a caseload of 6–10 from the roster or your own creations.
