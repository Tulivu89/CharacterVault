# Hallett College
> A first-year dorm at a magic college on the Maine coast, where everyone is paying for something and the building is older than the school admits.

*Untagged lines are canon. **[draft]** marks proposals waiting on approval.*

## Premise
Hallett College, in Wick Harbor, Maine, is an American magic university: corporatism, occultism and college drama, built from the Witch Cast visual bible. It's grounded, and it plays as the first year of college. Every student is an adult. Most of the cast are first years living on one floor of the only first-year dorm, along with an RA, two staff parents and the people they answer to.

The cast is designed so the drama runs without the protagonist. Every character has a want, a secret and a tie to at least two others. The protagonist arrives as a student in week three, into stories that are already moving.

## Tone
Campus drama first: money, grades, roommates, sex, families and secrets. The occult is real and mostly in the background: a lab injury, a withdrawn student, a door that stays locked. The Lovecraft comes in by the side door and is never the main event until it is.

Characters are built from their situation, not from sources. Each of the five original girls keeps one personality keystone, and nobody else has one.

## Core rules
- **Houses are sponsors, not dorms.** You're admitted *through* a house as a legacy, on a scholarship or under a sponsor contract. The house pays part of your tuition and expects something back.
  - **Abyssal** (deep navy): the sea, dreams, old money.
  - **Oxblood** (oxblood red): blood rites, ambition, sport.
  - **Verdigris** (verdigris green): archives, alchemy.
  - **Ashen Gold** (ochre gold): commerce, the sponsors' house.
- **Unaffiliated.** Staff children attend free on tuition remission, but they come in with no house sponsor. They eat at the overflow table.
- **Magic is a major.** PRAC 101, Foundations of Practice, is the weed-out course every first year takes. A third of the class drops it. Practicals injure people.
- **Money sorts people:** trusts, athletic scholarships, need-based aid with work-study, and sponsor contracts that come with strings.
- **Academic dress.** Worn to lectures, practicals and formal hall, never to the dining hall. Women wear the black hooded uniform dress (see the bible). **[draft]** Men wear a black hooded blazer, a white dress shirt and black slacks, with the same pentagram pendant and rune embroidery on the blazer.
- **Hurl.** Hallett's full-contact warded field sport, part rugby and part rite, with men's and women's teams. It's Oxblood's pride and Ashen Gold's money, and it's played in Lindqvist Stadium.

## The wider world
- The magical economy runs on reagents, and supply contracts are power. Lindqvist Reagents supplies every lab on campus.
- Hermetic orders, old families and corporations all hold standing with the college. An order's word can overrule a professor.

## Places
- **North Hall.** The only first-year dorm. It has four floors, coed by wing, with double rooms (two twin beds each), one bathroom per wing and an RA per floor. The cast lives on the third floor. Over the door, under NORTH HALL, YBARRA HALL shows through when the stone is wet.
- **The cottage.** The facilities supervisor's house, behind North Hall. Salt on the windowsills.
- **The steam tunnels.** They link North Hall's basement to the library and the science buildings, and they're locked. The lowest level ends at an iron door.
- **The library basement archive.** Admissions files, sealed disciplinary records and donor letters, staffed by work-study students.
- **Lindqvist Stadium.** Hurl, and the name of the family that pays for it.
- **The overflow table.** Where unaffiliated students eat.

## Peoples
- Students and staff are human. What's under the building may not be.

## Conflict axes
- **Legacy vs. scholarship vs. sponsor vs. unaffiliated:** who paid for you, and what they get back.
- **Heir vs. backup:** families who own their children's futures.
- **Secrets vs. the archive:** everything happened to someone, and someone filed it.
- **Students vs. the building:** the college keeps its dead quiet.

## Engines
*Each one runs without the protagonist.*
1. **The two heirs.** Julian Baek is back and readmitted into his sister Adeline's dorm. Grandmother Baek calls every Sunday and has started asking him about his grades too. Julian wants Adeline to have the inheritance, the family won't allow it, and Adeline thinks his kindness is pity.
2. **Junior year.** Adeline and Saga were best friends at boarding school until something both of them did. Wes's father drafted the NDA, and Cata has read the sealed note in the admissions files. Saga wants Adeline back, and Adeline won't look at her.
3. **The 2.5.** Dev's scholarship needs a 2.5 GPA, and she scored 54 on the first PRAC quiz. Her tutor is the professor's daughter, which nobody knows. Midterms are in week seven.
4. **Room 3B.** Saga is sleeping with the RA, a fireable offense, and he files reports to her father's company. Cata knows. Wes says goodnight to Cata when Saga's in the shower.
5. **The roommate report.** Dr. Bekele had housing room her daughter with Linh, and Selam reports on her. Linh knows, and she's running her own experiment on Selam. Selam is falling for her. Linh's other subject is Julian, the one person on the floor who has heard what she's looking for, and Selam has noticed.
6. **The tunnels.** Caleb Prentiss withdrew in week two, after asking Gabe how to get into the tunnels, and his things are still in his room. Linh goes down every night. Ray keeps the iron door shut. Julian hears something under the building that sounds like his practical.
7. **The name on the lintel.** Ray has worked here for 24 years and recognizes Cata's face from a photograph in the boiler room.

## Arc
1. **Move-in.** Rooms, roommates and the floor meeting.
2. **Night two.** Saga's party.
3. **Week two.** Caleb withdraws.
4. **Week three.** The protagonist arrives.
5. **Week four.** The first PRAC quiz.
6. **Week seven.** Midterms decide Dev's scholarship and the Baek family's choice.
7. **Halloween.** The big hurl game, and the night the tunnels are busiest.
8. **Thanksgiving.** Families.
9. **Finals.** Who doesn't come back after winter break.

## Protagonist slot
- **Fixed:** a man, an adult first-year student who arrives in week three and takes Caleb Prentiss's empty bed in Wes Harlan's room on the third floor of North Hall. Caleb's things are still on the shelf.
- **Open:** his house (or none), his money, why he's late, and whose stories he steps into first.

## Lore entries
- **North Hall / Ybarra Hall / the lintel:** The only first-year dorm. It was built as Ybarra Hall with Tomás Ybarra's money. After the Ybarra Inquiry the college cut his name off the lintel and renamed it, and the old letters show when the stone is wet.
- **PRAC 101 / Foundations / Foundations of Practice:** The first-year weed-out course, designed and taught by Dr. Almaz Bekele. A third of the class drops it. Quizzes are graded on the working, not the answer.
- **Hurl / the hurl team:** Hallett's full-contact warded field sport, with men's and women's teams. Oxblood recruits for it, and scholarships depend on playing. Home games are in Lindqvist Stadium.
- **The steam tunnels / the iron door:** Utility tunnels from North Hall's basement to the library and science buildings. Ray Thibodeau keeps them locked. The lowest level ends at an iron door that nobody admits opening.
- **Houses / sponsors / unaffiliated:** Admission comes through a house (Abyssal, Oxblood, Verdigris, Ashen Gold), which pays part of tuition and expects something back. Staff children on tuition remission have no house and eat at the overflow table.
- **Community reports:** The monthly wellbeing reports that sponsored RAs file on their residents. Ashen Gold's go to the Lindqvist Reagents student-success office.
