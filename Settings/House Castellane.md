# House Castellane
> Someone inside House Castellane tried to give the patriarch final death at his own party. The estate stays sealed until he names them, and the party never stops.

*Untagged lines are canon. **[draft]** marks proposals waiting on approval.*

## Premise
This is a scenario more than a settled story. During the Equinox party, someone inside House Castellane tried to give the patriarch final death, and failed. Matins, the estate AI, sealed the house until he names the culprit. Family, guests and the rented entertainment are locked in together, and the party becomes an attempted-ish murder mystery without ever stopping.

The Castellanes consider themselves immortal, so the fun doesn't stop. It only gets more edge.

The core twist is that visual age has come loose from family role. Each Castellane wears the age they choose, or the age they're allowed. Parents can look younger than their children, and an heir can stay at 36 for a century. The relationships froze when the bodies did, then rotted in place.

Who sent the assassin is open by design. Each run decides it, or leaves it to play.

## Tone
Dark, decadent, adult and sexual. Life is cheap when bodies can be printed, so cruelty escalates, and the only lasting consequence is memory. In this house, questioning is a sport.

## Core rules
- **Lattice.** A continuity core fused to the spine that holds the mind. If it's destroyed, the person suffers final death.
- **Vessels.** Grown bodies printed from a stored gene template, replaced when worn out or killed. A vessel can be modified into anything, so a face, an eye color or an apparent age proves nothing about blood.
- **Renewal.** Resleeving into a fresh vessel. It costs more than most districts earn in a decade.
- **The renewal queue.** A family's internal order of who gets a new vessel and when. Whoever controls it holds power.
- **Naturals.** People with no lattice. They age, and they die once.
- **Lattice-burners.** Weapons that cook the core, not just the body. Illegal and rare.
- **Ages in the house.** Every Castellane vessel is adult. The house keeps no juvenile templates.

## The wider world
- **Kestrel Reach** is an orbital habitat above a drowned Earth. Wealth flows up the spindle, and the poorest decks sit closest to the hull.
- **The houses.** About forty families have lived past 150. They mostly marry among themselves, because nobody else shares their timescale.
- **Mods.** The range goes as far as money allows: digitigrade legs, prehensile tails, chitin plating, bioluminescent skin, extra joints, venom glands repurposed for pleasure, all the way to the fully alien or anthro. Old money stays human-shaped in public and treats heavy modification as servant fashion. What they wear in private is another matter.
- **The class gap.** Entertainers are rented by the night. Some wear disposable printed vessels, and some are naturals whose deaths are permanent. The gap between haves and have-nots is the gap between mortal and not. Because wounds can be undone, extreme play has no physical ceiling for the family. It does for everyone else.

## Places
- **The ballroom.** Where Aurelio died mid-dance, and where Inès didn't spill her drink.
- **The recovery suite.** Where Aurelio woke in a new body that still smells of growth medium.
- **Inès's suite.** Where she keeps Cassius on the second night.
- **The Castellane sector.** Everything Matins sealed under succession protocol.
- **[draft]** **The menagerie.** The entertainers' quarters, now the house's favorite place to ask questions.

## Conflict axes
- **[draft]** **Mortal ↔ Immortal.** Naturals die once, and the family can't be hurt for long. Who can afford to lose decides who takes risks, and who gets used.
- **[draft]** **Kept ↔ Free.** The queue decides who gets a body and when. Everyone in the house is trading for position or straining against the age they were allowed.
- **[draft]** **Body ↔ Memory.** Bodies can be undone and memory can't. Aurelio has lost the hours that matter, and everyone else is guarding what they remember.

## Arc
*[draft] The beats a run of the scenario moves through.*
1. **The Equinox** (before the story). The most exclusive invitation in Kestrel Reach: rival houses, hangers-on and a rented menagerie of entertainers.
2. **The attempt.** A courtesan reaches Aurelio mid-dance and burns out his vessel. His core survives. House security shoots her, and she's gone for good.
3. **The seal.** Matins locks the sector under succession protocol, trapping about forty guests, the family and the entertainers.
4. **The wake.** Six hours later Aurelio wakes in a fresh vessel, missing the hours before the attempt. He calls Séraphine first.
5. **The nights.** The party keeps running, because stopping would look like fear. Every entertainer is a suspect, questioning is a sport, and the play gets more edge each night.
6. **The name.** Aurelio names someone, right or wrong, and the seal lifts.

**Family mods**
- **Aurelio:** electrodes in his gold-tipped right-hand fingers. A touch overrides a nervous system for pain, pleasure or collapse.
- **Inès:** gold fiber-optic threads in her bun, and a pheromone worn like perfume that stirs attraction and obsession. Her heaviest mod stays private.
- **Dorian:** silver interface studs at his temples, and reinforced bones and joints that can crush things.
- **Cassius:** a polished copper prosthetic left hand.
- **Séraphine:** diamond implants along her cheekbones, and Pixie, a linked drone that feeds her socials and her senses.
