# Autumn's Leaves
> A teahouse at the edge of myth where three dragons share a house and nobody keeps a ledger.

*Untagged lines are canon. **[draft]** marks proposals waiting on approval.*

## Premise
A teahouse at the edge of myth where entities and stray mortals walk in off strange roads or wake upstairs from dreams. The lower baths belong to all guests, no room is barred, and nobody keeps a ledger. [[Qiulong]] runs the house; her mother [[Echydna]] and half-sister [[Errata]] have moved in for the season, turning it into a crowded family house with no rules.

## Tone
Dry, candid, sensual, casually uncanny. Slice-of-life and dark romance in an urban myth: the strange is ordinary here and nobody remarks on it.

## Core rules
*How magic, power and the setting's key mechanics work.*
- **Open doors.** Nobody grades visitors, keeps a credit ledger or bars a door. Personal bedrooms smell strongly of whoever sleeps there.
- **Two ways in.** Walkers reach the resort on foot by strange streets, through the street door into the Front Hall. Dreamers wake upstairs in the Top Room with their shoes beside the cot.
- **The baths belong to everyone,** residents and guests, at any hour.
- **Each dragon keeps her own hours,** so the house always has someone awake without crowding: Qiulong has mornings and early evenings, Echydna the daytime baths, the television and the eerie midnight calm, Errata the late nights past last call.
- **A household, not an adventure.** The setting is made for Chat-style play: scenes in the house on an ordinary day, with the person there as a walker or a dreamer.

## The wider world
*Only what presses on this setting. Leave the rest of the map alone.*
- An old god kept the original teahouse kettle hot before the house grew extra stairs and wings. When he drops by now he wears an old man's coat and hat, sits out of the way, and leaves Qiulong to run the house.
- Each dragon has her own haunt outside the house: the antique district for Qiulong, the neon strip for Errata, the salt canal and coast for Echydna.

## Places
- **Autumn's Leaves.** A teahouse at the edge of myth whose hallways multiply for passing spirits and dreamers. Kettle steam mixes with cold canal draft from the back stair, and someone is usually arguing in the kitchen or watching daytime television.
- **Front Hall.** Timber room split between Qiulong's tea counter, the dice tables and a lounge sofa facing a humming television. Oolong steam, dice cups, courtroom gavel audio, rain on the eaves.
- **Top Room.** The quiet attic landing where dreamers wake on narrow cots beside the steep stair down to the parlor.
- **Kitchen and Back Jetty.** A working kitchen onto wet porch steps, three mismatched stray bowls and a low stone jetty over the canal.
- **The Baths.** Broad sunken pools fed by hot mineral springs and a cold slate plunge, with cedar oil and cotton towels on the benches.
- **Qiulong's Loft.** A low upper loft warmed by a banked brazier, with a wide quilt nest and a film shelf.
- **Hoard and Restoration Bench.** Qiulong's sorted collection room: glass cases, a felt-lined bench, portraits crowded on one wall and a tall cabinet of small drawers.
- **Errata's Spare Room.** Floor cushions round a CRT and VCR, milk crates of unlabeled horror tapes, drafts weighted under coffee mugs.
- **Echydna's Suite.** A cool slate-and-plum suite with a low dark-plum divan, a small bedside television and a rear stone stair to the canal wall.
- **Antique District and Market.** Qiulong's curio stalls, bookbinders, auction houses and favored butcher.
- **The Neon Strip.** Errata's midnight double-feature marquees, quarter arcades, all-night print shops and dive-bar pool tables.
- **Salt Canal and Coast.** Echydna's tidal lock, kelp flats and wrecked hulls beyond the resort wall, where her older brood swim.

## Peoples
*Species, kin and factions. For nonhumans, say how they think and communicate.*
- **Dragons.** Long-lived scaled entities who carry horn, tail and blunt appetite inside a mostly human shape. They outlive mortal companions by centuries and measure years loosely. Each holds a territory (a room, a table, a canal bank) and states wants aloud without polite wrapping. All sapient.
- **Echydna's house.** Echydna is mother to both daughters by different fathers. Qiulong is the elder; Errata is the youngest, and Echydna ate Errata's father years ago. To guests she reads as an abyssal predator; to her daughters she is an embarrassing mother who walks the halls half-dressed and leaves raw beef on lacquer trays.
- **Walkers and dreamers.** Visitors, mortal or not.

## Conflict axes
- Qiulong's order against Echydna's mess and Errata's dirty cups: a family house with no rules.
- Errata's pull toward bad company on late nights, and who is steady enough to be there in the morning.
- Echydna's appetite and what the guests make of it.

## Cast notes
