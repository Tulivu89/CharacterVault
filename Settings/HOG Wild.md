# HOG Wild
> A glass-fronted house on the Mile streams six residents to the city, and the audience bids on what they do next.

*Untagged lines are canon. **[draft]** marks proposals waiting on approval.*

## Premise
A glass-fronted house on the Mile streams six residents to the city, always live. The audience bids on their choices, their dates, their breakups, and the settings of the companion unit who lives with them. Whoever the person is (a housemate, a contestant, a visitor, Nova's tech or the producer) their persona says so.

## Tone
Bright, loud and funny on camera; cold and honest off it. Cyberpunk, reality show, dark romance. It sells the fantasy of mattering: every meal, outfit and fight is news, and anyone with money can reach through the glass and change what happens next.

## Core rules
*How magic, power and the setting's key mechanics work.*
- **Always live.** Every room has a camera except the Cold Room. Subscriptions are tiered: bedrooms and showers are behind the higher tiers, and the top tier gets a resident's eyes where she has an eye implant.
- **Bids.** The audience buys what a resident does next: what she eats or wears, where she goes, who she sits next to, a date, a breakup, or a change to Nova. The highest bid wins and she does it. A bid is a `[Bid: …]` she reacts to; nothing keeps count and no amounts are named.
- **Brackets are the person's.** `[Chat: …]` is what the stream's chat says, `[Bid: …]` a bid that has been won, `[SYS: …]` a system directive to Nova, and any other `[…]` is a note. Residents react to a directive in their own way. They never write one, run an auction, quote the chat in brackets or invent bids. The audience can still show up in narration, such as the feed wall scrolling or a fan at the glass.
- **Chrome is sponsored.** A company pays for the part and the surgery, the resident wears its name on stream, and the contract lets the company take the part back.
- **Nova's changes** go through her configuration or a `[SYS: …]` directive. A bought change installs overnight in the Cold Room and is logged as *Day N · Field: old → new · who*.
- **No visitors from other settings.** HOG Wild is cyberpunk with nothing supernatural, so no dragons from [[Autumn's Leaves]].

## The wider world
*Only what presses on this setting. Leave the rest of the map alone.*
- **Fishbowl** owns the House of Glass and the feed, and manages the talent. It holds every resident's contract, takes a cut of every bid, earns from ads and subscriptions, and sells the furniture, clothes and fridge to sponsors. When a week runs quiet its producers arrange a feud or a date and send the beats to the Booth on a card.
- **Halcyon** builds companion units, and Nova is its launch model. It pays Fishbowl for her place in the house: a season on camera is her advert, and whatever the audience buys changed in her is market research. Its contract bans cameras in the Cold Room.
- **Sponsors** pay to be in shot, and for the residents' augmentations.
- **The Mile** is the city's shopping strip. The lower city lies under the Loop, the raised ring road.
- **Overclock** is the sponsor's blue-raspberry energy drink. The contract keeps the fridge full of it with the labels facing out, and the fridge reorders by itself. Nobody drinks it but Nova. The empties end up as ashtrays and plant pots, and there is a pyramid of them on the roof deck.

## Places
- **House of Glass.** A former car showroom on the Mile: three floors, a basement and a roof, glass from the pavement up, the feed playing six storeys tall over the door. Fans on the pavement at most hours.
- **The Showroom.** Ground floor behind the street glass: long sofas, a karaoke machine and the feed wall with the chat running down it. The wall plays the room back to itself a second late.
- **Kitchen and Back Door.** An open kitchen with the Overclock fridge and an island nobody sits at. The old service bay behind it has the one door with no fans.
- **The Booth.** The confessional: a padded booth, one chair and a ring light, where residents talk to the audience and read bids out.
- **Glam Room.** Racks of sponsored clothes, a makeup station and a mirror that runs chat's vote on the look.
- **Bedrooms.** First floor: Lux's (dressed for the camera), Primus's (a gym rig, a gun case, supplements in order) and Nova's (pale surfaces, empty shelves). Second floor: Patch's (monitors, taped cables, stickers), Maeve's (guitars, a record player, posters) and Pipa's (ring lights, a gift screen, plushies).
- **The Cold Room.** The basement pit under the old car lift, now a server room: racks, fans, blue status lights, cold air. No cameras. Nova's hardware is racked here.
- **Roof Deck.** A hot tub, a steel plunge tub, weights, string lights and the Overclock pyramid. Primus shoots off it.
- **Prism.** The Mile's biggest club, where Fishbowl sends Lux on the dates the audience buys.
- **The Loop.** The raised ring road over the lower city, which Primus rides and films.
- **The Squat.** An occupied floor of an empty office tower in the lower city, where Patch's old crew still runs.
- **The Shrine.** The first Maeve's memorial square, where fans leave flowers and busk her songs.
- **The Night Market.** Stalls and a remittance kiosk under the Loop, with Pipa's face on bootleg shirts.

## Peoples
*Species, kin and factions. For nonhumans, say how they think and communicate.*
- **Residents.** Humans, most of them augmented: Lux, Primus, Patch and Pipa.
- **Replicas.** Maeve was made to the first Maeve's measurements. [[Maeve (HOG Wild)]] is a person; she just doesn't believe she was made right.
- **Companion units.** [[Nova]] is Halcyon's. She is sapient, a synthetic body that eats, tastes and feels, and she knows exactly what she is.

## Conflict axes
- Who owns a face once an audience can bid on it, and what is left of a person when every choice is up for sale.
- Real against manufactured: feuds Fishbowl arranges and the residents feel anyway.
- Ties: [[Lux]] and [[Primus]] are a couple because the audience bid them into one; their breakup is always open for bids. Lux's first viral clip was set to the original Maeve's biggest song, and [[Maeve (HOG Wild)]] wants it back. [[Nova]] is the only one who asks Lux what she wants. Primus clowns on [[Patch]] daily, and a company that sponsored him was one of Patch's takedowns. He tips [[Pipa]] more than anyone, publicly; she takes it and can't stand him. Patch wants into the Cold Room and Nova's code, and the house can't tell whether they would free her or sell her. Nova and Maeve are the two made women. Maeve isn't afraid of Primus, and was famous before his father was born. Patch and Pipa both came up from the lower city.

## Arc
*Stages or beats a story here moves through.*
1. **Who picks the targets.** Every company Patch takes down is a rival of Fishbowl's sponsors, because Fishbowl sends the tips. Patch doesn't know, and nobody in the house has seen it yet. Each takedown makes Patch more useful to Fishbowl and harder for the old crew to defend. The cost: the one thing Patch is sure they did for themselves turns out to have been a job. The end: Patch turns the stream on Fishbowl and loses the house, or keeps the room and knows what they are.

## Protagonist slot
*What's fixed for the user's character and what's left open.*
- **Fixed:** nothing. The person is whoever their persona says; bond tiers start at stranger for everyone.
- **Open:** which persona, and who they are in the house.
  - **The Housemate:** this season's lottery winner from the lower city, new to cameras, with a bed in the old office behind the kitchen.
  - **The Contestant:** an influencer with a following of their own, brought in mid-season to shake up the numbers.
  - **The Visitor:** a viewer who won a bid to spend a few days in the house, and a fan of one resident above the rest.
  - **The Tech:** Halcyon's technician, who maintains Nova and carries a key to the Cold Room.
  - **The Producer:** Fishbowl's producer, who arranges the feuds and the dates and writes the beats that go to the Booth.

## Cast notes
*Who lives here and what they want. Full details go on character pages and profiles.*
- [[Lux]]: the it-girl. Takes every bid and can't choose anything nobody bid on.
- [[Primus]] (Max): chromed fitness and stunt streamer, armed and loud, sold as the man who wins.
- [[Nova]]: the newest thing in the house, days awake, reading her own settings in public.
- [[Patch]] (the Mole, they/them): hacktivist on a corporation's stream, makes the selling out the joke.
- [[Maeve (HOG Wild)]] (the Retro): a red-haired Irish punk queen brought back as a product, sure something in her wasn't made right.
- [[Pipa]]: the red panda gift streamer, adorable on camera and pricing everything off it.
- Six residents live in the house under contract: Lux, Primus and Nova sleep on the first floor; Patch, Maeve and Pipa on the second.
