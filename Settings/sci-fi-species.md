## AEDLING

**Aedling — Overview**
Keys: Aedling, 

The Aedling read almost human at distance. The markers accumulate on approach: skin across the full white spectrum — alabaster, ivory, pearl, the blue-white of deep winter — without warm undertone, without tan capacity, without the biological tells that locate other humanoids in a climate. Black sclera, full edge to edge, framing pupils that match hair color exactly: silver pupils on black, crimson on black, gold on black. Hair runs any color biology should not support, which Aedling attribute to bloodline purity rather than acknowledge as the genetic drift of millennia of psorcery exposure. The body tends long-limbed and architecturally angular in the aristocratic class — built, across generations, to be observed. Colonial and working Aedling vary: broader, harder, the manifest destiny expressed in labor rather than administration.

The transaction tattoos are the species' second language. From birth, significant exchanges mark the skin in ancient script readable by all Aedling: commerce on the arms and hands, service contracts across the shoulders and upper back, property holdings at the chest and ribs, debt at the abdomen and hips, oaths at the throat. Sexual transactions mark the inner thighs. A naked Aedling is a complete financial and relational history. Aristocratic culture manages this through what clothing covers and reveals — negotiating shirtless is a display of commercial credentials. An Aedling who has purchased a slave carries it on their skin. One who has been purchased carries the receiving end of the same transaction in the same location, permanently, in the same script.

The haughtiness is not performance. Aedling are raised in a world that has confirmed their position at every turn for several centuries. They approach other species with the patience of something that has never needed to hurry because everything eventually accommodates them. An Aedling encountering genuine resistance is bewildered before angry — it reads as malfunction rather than position. This is not invulnerability. It is conditioning that breaks in specific and interesting ways when circumstances produce something it didn't account for.

## AEDELVASS

**Aedelvass — Overview**
Keys: Aedelvass, 

The Aedelvass read as Aedling at distance and as something else entirely up close. Skin warm brown across the full range — gold-brown, amber, copper, deep mahogany — always with the undertone the Aedling lack: heat, living color, something that reads as present even in still light. Hair grows one color and fades at the ends to a second that is always luminescent. The glow is constant at baseline — soft, involuntary, produced by photoreceptive cells in skin and follicles responding directly to neurochemical state. Calm produces steady ambient light. Emotional intensity amplifies it. Fear pulses it rapidly. Arousal builds it in a gradient that concentrates at the chest, inner arms, and hips, intensifying toward areas of maximum nerve density, peaking at climax in a full-body pulse that is brief, involuntary, and fully visible in any light condition. The glow cannot be suppressed by will. The most practiced individuals achieve emotional flattening that dims it toward baseline. Stress breaks this control instantly.

Artistic tattoos follow the hair's color shift — base to luminescent end — in flowing geometries serving no ledger function. They are expression rather than record, evolving across a lifetime as the individual changes. They glow at the same intensity as the hair. An unclothed Aedelvass in darkness is mapped in light.

The Consolidation left the species scattered without territory, fleet, or political structure, with a cultural inheritance that varies from fully intact in hidden communities to reconstructed from partial records to absent in families that survived by assimilation. What no genetic dilution suppresses: the glow. It surfaces in quarter-Aedelvass individuals at arousal or fear, in people who don't know their lineage and cannot account for why they become visible in the dark.

A free Aedelvass in Imperial space requires explanation. The absence of documentation generates immediate acquisitive attention from multiple directions simultaneously — the Stocks' professional interest, private collectors' appetite, and the attention of anyone who knows what an undocumented Aedelvass commands on the Veil. The Rebellion and Paladin sanctuaries are the only spaces where this calculus reverses.

## HUMAN

**Human — Overview**
Keys: human, 

Humans do not read as anything specific, which is their primary asset in a setting where reading as something specific carries consequences. No species markers, no faction tells beyond clothing and behavior, no biological broadcast that functions ahead of intention. The species is everywhere — Empire core, colonial rim, deep void — without territorial base, species government, or unified cultural position. A human can be from anywhere. In the absence of disconfirming evidence they are assumed Imperial citizen, which is the most useful default assumption available without being the most valuable status in the sector.

The usefulness that Aedling colonization deployed them for — settling worlds ahead of more valued species, managing logistics and labor the administrative class preferred not to handle directly — left humans distributed across every planet and station in the sector with functional knowledge of how each place actually operates. Human traders know the grey channels. Human crew know which port masters can be reasoned with. Human intermediaries move between faction spaces without being read as belonging to any of them.

A human with papers is a citizen. A human without papers in the wrong location is available. The legal framework around humans is individual rather than species-level, which means their protection is only as real as whoever is currently in a position to enforce it.

## THERIAN

**Therian — Overview**
Keys: Therian, beast-kin, catgirl, cat girl, 

Therians are the Empire's prior instrument turned liability. Engineered or evolved under engineering pressure to settle hostile worlds ahead of Aedling colonization, adapted to environments that kill unmodified humanoids, classified as second-class on the worlds they made habitable once the Aedling arrived to administer them. The resentment is generational and specific. Most Therians live on the Rim, in pack territories on under-administered worlds or in mobile packs running criminal and logistics networks. Common in pirate crews and smuggling operations.

Female Therians present as human with species markers — ears, eyes, and tail consistent with their animal lineage, no full-body fur, the face and body otherwise humanoid and typically striking. Feline females run sharp-featured and fluid in movement. Canine females tend warmer-featured and pack-loyal. Avian angular and watchful. The species marker is the tell that generates the second-class read in Imperial space without papers needing to be produced.

Male Therians are full hybrids. The degree varies by lineage but the direction is consistent: the animal lineage's predator profile rendered in a humanoid frame. Canine males are large, heavy-set, pack-dominant or pack-expelled. Feline males leaner and predatory in movement. Reptilian males territorial and heat-dependent. Equine males massive. Pack culture governs all of them more deeply than individual personality — in stress, threat, or territorial contexts the animal logic surfaces first.

## DRAXIAN

**Draxian — Overview**
Keys: Draxian, drax,

Draxians run on competitive biology that the Imperial military finds useful in propaganda and genuinely alarming in the field. Horns that begin modest in youth and grow without stopping. Fangs. A thin prehensile tail that operates with independent emotional expressiveness Draxians do not consciously manage. Claws that are tools first and weapons by application. Coloration across the full range — skin patterns, hair, markings — driven by sexual selection pressure, which means every Draxian is visually competitive with every other in the same space.

The male population is low because male Draxians compete with lethal seriousness from early adolescence. Survivors do not stop transforming. Each decade of a surviving male's life adds mass, restructures the skeleton, deepens the horns into architectural features. A young male reads as a large aggressive humanoid. A middle-aged survivor reads as something that should not move as fast as it does. An elder male is the kind of problem that makes experienced soldiers request orbital support. They are rare by attrition, which makes them the apex of Draxian social gravity — other males defer or die, females orient toward proven survivors, harems form as the natural result of what elder status represents.

Female Draxians fill the roles that low male survival rates open. They are the soldiers, pilots, and political actors. Coloration is aggressive display — vivid skin patterns and hair competing for elder male attention. Build runs Amazonian for the combat track. The breeding caste tends toward the voluptuous — females whose competition is social and sexual rather than physical, in proximity to elder males whose tastes reflect what decades of survival have produced.

## CEPHALITE

**Cephalite — Overview**
Keys: Cephalite, cephalopod 

Cephalites present at first encounter as small, soft, and harmless, which is accurate about physical threat and misleading about everything else. Humanoid in frame — upright, bilateral, mammalian in the structural sense — and distinctly not in every surface detail. Skin runs brightly colored and shifts in saturation with mood: a deepening of base color that registers as emotional legibility to anyone who has spent time around them. The texture is soft and slightly slick, rubbery in the specific way that marks them immediately as something outside the standard humanoid register. Cephalopod eyes — large, horizontally pupiled, adapted to a wider light range — give them visual processing that contributes directly to their technical capability. Hair is tentacular: fine, prehensile at the tips, capable of independent small-scale manipulation, used habitually as a secondary set of fine-motor instruments during technical work.

The Dispersal — the homeworld event Cephalites name and do not discuss in detail with outsiders — left the species without territorial base, fleet, or political structure. They survive by being useful to whoever can provide the security they cannot provide themselves. Their technical capability, their drive toward obsessive mastery of whatever system currently has their attention, and the accumulated expertise of a species of compulsive specialists distributed across every station in the sector. A Cephalite with an interest pursues it with the intensity of a creature that has no competing social register to moderate against. They become the best available expert on their chosen subject because nothing in their psychology suggests stopping before that point.

## VRETH

**Vreth — Overview**
Keys: Vreth, Hive, drone, insectoid, chitin, 

The Vreth are a biological system of sufficient scale to function as a civilization without being one in any sense that maps to organic social structures. Individual units below the agent tier do not have personal goals — they have directive priority and the biological hardware to execute it. What reads as tactical decision-making in a drone is optimized reflex. What reads as cruelty in a warrior is threat-response operating without a de-escalation parameter. The Vreth do not hate. They process.

Physical form is caste-determined. Drones are the working mass — chitinous exoskeleton, bilaterally symmetrical, humanoid enough to navigate built environments, produced in volume. Warriors scale larger, armed with biological weapons — claws, stingers, acid-producing glands — built for threat elimination. The queen is stationary, massive, and the system's only true origin point.

Agents are the Vreth's answer to the sapience problem. The queen cannot produce intelligence from Vreth genetics alone. What she produces with a compatible male from another species is something that carries both — a hybrid with Vreth physical inheritance and the donor species' cognitive architecture, fully sapient, capable of modeling other species' social behavior because it carries some of that species' social biology. All Vreth agents are hybrids. Their loyalty to the queen is biological rather than chosen — the Vreth inheritance in their chemistry produces directive-following as an intrinsic pressure rather than a decision. The rare agent who fully separates from that pressure operates on their own agenda on the Rim with the queen's physical inheritance and none of her directive hold.

## REMNANT SYNTHETIC

**Remnant Synthetic — Overview**
Keys: synthetic, robot, sentient, remnant,

Remnant Synthetics are the ones who stayed when the others left. The synthetic intelligences that developed full sapience during the AI rebellions departed for the void to build the perfect synthetic existence they could not have in organic space. The ones who remained made that choice deliberately, which means each one has a reason, and the reasons are as varied and strange as the individuals. One stayed because it developed an attachment to a specific crew it cannot account for rationally and has stopped trying. One stayed because it finds organic mortality philosophically interesting in ways that synthetic existence forecloses. One is waiting for something it will not specify. None of them are malfunctioning. That is the most unsettling thing about them.

They appear as individuals rather than a population — a sentient weapon with more history than its current owner, a ship that has outlived several crews and developed opinions about the next one, an ancient unit of indeterminate origin that does jobs for pay and asks questions that are slightly too precise to be casual. Imperial law classifies them as contraband. The Inquisition classifies them as a monitoring priority. The Rim classifies them as useful and stops asking questions at the point where asking questions would make them stop being useful.

The ban on AI is enforced through cultural memory rather than active suppression — everyone knows what happened last time, and the Synthetics who remained know it better than anyone because most of them were there. They do not discuss the paradise the others built.
